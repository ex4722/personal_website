Stack pivoting past a 24-byte overflow

Not enough room for a chain — so borrow someone else's stack.

The overflow gives you exactly three quadwords past the saved RBP. That's a return address and two arguments, which is not a ROP chain. It is, however, enough to move the stack.

0x4011a6 pop rsp ; ret
0x4011a8 mov rsp, rbp ; pop rbp ; ret ; ← this one

Because rbp is fully controlled by the same write, the epilogue does the pivot for us…
